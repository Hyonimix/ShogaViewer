# Privacy Policy for Shoga Viewer

**Effective Date:** May 6, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy applies to the Shoga Viewer Chrome Extension ("the Extension"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information remains secure. This policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
Shoga Viewer is designed to process data primarily on your local device. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or telemetry to our servers or any third parties.** All media processing, rendering, and video upscaling/transcoding are performed locally on your machine.

## 3. Permissions and Local Storage
The Extension requests the following permissions to function correctly:
* **Storage**: The Extension uses standard browser storage APIs (`localStorage` and `IndexedDB`) to save your application settings, recent directories, and bookmarks. This data is stored securely and strictly locally on your device.
* **Context Menus**: The Extension uses this permission to provide a convenient right-click option ("Open media with Shoga Viewer") within your browser.
* **Host Permissions (`http://*/*` and `https://*/*`)**: Used strictly to allow dynamic network requests to your custom, undefined Jellyfin media server URL. The Extension only communicates with the specific server you manually input.
* **File System Access**: The Extension accesses your local files and directories solely when you explicitly select them. The Extension only reads the files to display or process them locally and does not upload them to the internet.

## 4. Jellyfin Integration
When connecting to a personal Jellyfin server, your server URL, username, and password are used exclusively to authenticate and communicate directly with your specified server. The Extension itself does not store or cache your credentials or authentication tokens; you may choose to save your login details using your browser's native password manager. This data is never transmitted to the developer or any tracking services.

## 5. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
